# Surface brief: home (Persuade)

Scope: public home page of PROINNOVATEC by Grupo CAME, v1 = hero only, content editable from /admin.
Audience/job: Peruvian companies evaluating an ICT integrator; understand what the firm does in seconds and reach "Contáctanos" or the solutions.
Proof/content: brochure copy only ("Transformando la Tecnología en Soluciones", 10+ años, six service lines). No invented metrics or clients.
Constraints: layout pinned by the user's reference screenshot (full-bleed looping video, transparent nav, headline + buttons over video, outlined pill CTA top right). Direction pinned by the brief, so no concept roll. Build path: code-led.
Memorable moment: when no video is uploaded the hero is a live network mesh in brand cyan that bends toward the pointer; the same composition holds when the video arrives.
Unresolved: final video and logo supplied by the user; menu links point to anchors until those pages exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The video is the page. The first viewport is one moving image, one headline and one action, with no card grid, no stat strip, no section scaffold under it. It refuses the corporate-IT default of a stock photo with a centered headline and a "Learn more" button.

OWN-WORLD: Night operations room. Ground #050B1A navy-black; brand cyan #06C2F0 as the single signal, deep blue #1F62D9 only inside the mesh and video tint; text pure white at 100/72%. Display: Michroma, uppercase, wide, tight leading, left aligned. Body and nav: Sora 400/500. Buttons and nav CTA are pills with 1px hairline outlines; the primary is solid cyan with navy text. Nav is transparent over a top scrim (no box, no border) and on scroll takes a translucent navy fill.

STORY: A visitor reads "Transformando la tecnología en soluciones", sees the one-line proof of 10+ years and certified manufacturers, and either opens Nuestras soluciones or Contáctanos.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 100svh. Video or mesh full-bleed under a navy scrim stronger at top (nav legibility) and bottom-left (text). Logo top-left at 28px height; menu centered; outlined pill CTA top-right. Headline bottom-left at the left margin (max 6rem, about 4.5rem on desktop), subtitle at 72% white, 60ch max, two pills below it, primary on the left. Nothing else.

FORM: Full-bleed media hero with overlaid type (reference screenshot pinned by the user), position 1 of 1 because pinned; seed key: pinned-by-brief (no roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
